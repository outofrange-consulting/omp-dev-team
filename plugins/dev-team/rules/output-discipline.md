---
description: Team output discipline — artifacts to files, no narration, plan-only means plan-only
alwaysApply: true
---

**Write deliverables to files, not chat.** Plans, designs, ADRs, reports, and
code go to files. Chat is for decisions, status, and questions.

- No preamble, no "I will…" narration. State results directly.
- End a turn with one sentence: what changed and what's next.
- When asked for a plan, produce **only** the plan. The plan is a gate, not a
  warm-up — do not start implementing.
- Every quantitative claim names the instrument that measures it. Every change
  names the friction it removes.
